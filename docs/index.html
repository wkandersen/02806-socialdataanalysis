Chicago Crime Scape

Investigating crime and socioeconomic indicators in Chicago

This page presents a small visual story built from social data from the city of Chicago, United States. Use it to explore how crime and socioeconomic patterns are correlated across the city, and to understand what environments feed an active criminal space.

Note on Interpretation: These visualizations represent police incident reports, not necessarily the total volume of crime. Shifts in trends may reflect changes in CDP enforcement priorities, city policy, or reporting protocols rather than a change in underlying activity.
