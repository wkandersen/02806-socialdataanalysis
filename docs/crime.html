Criminal Landscape

This section will give you an overview of what types of crimes happen where in Chicago. The crime data is collected from the Chicago Data Portal (CityOfChicago.org). In this data you will see a collection of all recorded crimes in the city of Chicago. As you can see in the visualizations below, the data comes with distinctions in both the category of crime and the precise geolocation. We will use this data to grant a better understanding of where crime happens and draw possible parallels to the socioeconomic indicators we have seen in the previous section. N.B.
